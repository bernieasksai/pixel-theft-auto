# Pixel Theft Auto

A single-file GTA2-style top-down action game you can play in the browser.

**Play it here:** https://bernieasksai.github.io/pixel-theft-auto/

## Controls

### Keyboard and mouse
- **WASD** or arrow keys — move or steer
- **Mouse** — aim and shoot
- **E**, **F**, or **Enter** — enter or exit cars, shops, and other prompts
- **R** — rob, mug, or intimidate
- **Space** — handbrake in a car (also starts the game from the title screen)
- **Shift** — sprint on foot, or nitro in a car if it is installed
- **Q** or **Tab** — cycle weapons
- **1–7** — select a weapon
- **C** — phone (GPS, jobs, bank, rep, and save)
- **I** — bag / inventory
- **P** or **Esc** — pause
- **M** — mute

### Touch
On a phone or tablet, on-screen controls appear automatically: a move joystick on the left, an aim stick on the right, contextual action buttons, and phone, bag, pause, and fullscreen at the top.
